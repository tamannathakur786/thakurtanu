The Soul of Riwaaz

"Riwaaz" (रिवाज़) represents custom, tradition, and timeless ceremony. We exist to celebrate the sacred thread of Indian textile heritage, reimagined for the contemporary woman.

OUR GENESIS

A Love Affair with Handloom & Royal Needlework

Riwaaz Clothing was born out of a yearning for authenticity in an era of mass-manufactured fast fashion. We traveled through the artisan clusters of Jaipur, Varanasi, and Lucknow to bring back the lost luxury of slow, conscientious handloom weaving.

Every Riwaaz ensemble takes anywhere from 40 to 180 karigar hours to complete. From the hand-chiseling of wooden blocks in Bagru to the placement of delicate zardozi cords and scalloped gota patti borders, our outfits carry human warmth and heritage dignity.

OUR ETHOS

The Four Pillars of Riwaaz

Pure Organic Textiles

We strictly shun polyester blends. Every garment is crafted from authentic Chanderi, pure Raw Silk, 60s Cambric Cotton, or plush 9000 Micro Velvet with cotton mulmul linings.

Master Karigari

Our needlework honors authentic Jaipur Gota Patti, Lucknowi Resham embroidery, and Kashmiri Tilla threadwork executed with heirloom precision.

Artisan Direct Fair-Trade

By eliminating middlemen, we pay fair dignified wages directly to generational weaver families, preserving ancient Indian textile arts.
