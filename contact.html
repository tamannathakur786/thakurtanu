Frequently Asked Questions

How does ordering via WhatsApp work?

When you click "Order on WhatsApp" or checkout from your bag, WhatsApp opens with your chosen garments, sizes, and price pre-filled directly to our team at +91 8580540470. We verify availability, share payment links (UPI, Cards, NetBanking), and send real-time courier tracking numbers.

What fabrics do you use in your outfits?

We work exclusively with pure natural textiles: Handwoven Chanderi Silk, Raw Silk, 100% Bagru 60s Cambric Cotton, Fluid Georgette Crepe, Sheer Organza Silk, and 9000 Micro Velvet. Every silk garment is lined with soft cotton mulmul.

Can I request custom sleeve lengths or plus sizes?

Yes! Customization is our pride. We can customize sleeve cuts, kurta length, and sizes up to 5XL. Simply message us on WhatsApp at 8580540470 with your measurements.

What is your return and size exchange policy?

We offer a 7-day hassle-free size exchange. Simply message our WhatsApp desk at 8580540470 with your order ID, and we arrange a reverse pickup!

Do you offer Cash on Delivery (COD)?

Yes, Cash on Delivery is available across major pincodes in India. We also provide secure online payments via UPI, Google Pay, PhonePe, credit/debit cards, and NetBanking.
